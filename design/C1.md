# C1 — Jetpack refuels on the ground

Status: docs. Feedback 5 (first playtest, 2026-10-17).

## What and why

Today the tank is one-shot: 100 units per life, burned at `FUEL_BURN_RATE`
while thrusting, and nothing in the game refills it. The text promises a dock
station that does not exist. Playtest: once the tank is dry the player is
stuck at the bottom of whatever they dug, and the run is over in all but name.

The fuel economy is removed for now. While the unit stands on the ground the
tank refills at `FUEL_BURN_RATE` per frame up to 100. The tank still limits how
long one climb lasts, so the jetpack keeps its shape as a resource; it just no
longer limits a whole life.

## Proposed behaviour

One rule in `updatePlayer`, next to the existing clamp:

```
grounded at the start of the frame and not thrusting this frame
  → fuel += FUEL_BURN_RATE, clamped to 100
```

- Rate is the burn rate: one frame on the ground pays back one frame of
  flight. No new constant.
- `grounded` is the value left by the previous frame's move — the same value
  that already picks `FOOT_POWER` over `JETPAK_POWER` at `player.js:172`.
- No refill while thrusting, so holding `up` under a ceiling does not net to
  zero and the rule stays "ground refills, flight burns".
- Thrust still requires `fuel > 0`; one grounded frame after empty is enough
  to lift again.

## Edge cases

- **Grounded in liquid.** Standing on the floor of a pool sets `grounded`
  (`inLiquid` does not block it), so the tank refills underwater. Floating or
  swimming is not grounded and does not refill. Open question 2.
- **Sandbox.** No burn, fuel stays 100; regen adds nothing. No special case.
- **`dryTank` flag.** Today it is sticky for the life of a parcel and only
  feeds the one-shot `dry_tank` milestone. With regen an empty tank is a
  momentary state, not the end of flight.
- **Buried.** The unstick loop (`player.js:228+`) moves the unit after the
  ground check; a buried unit resting on material is grounded and refills.
  Harmless.
- **Arrival drop.** The unit spawns in the air with 100; no effect.
- **Infinite world.** `shiftPlayer` moves x only; fuel is untouched.
- **Fractional fuel.** Values stay multiples of 0.5 between 0 and 100; the
  HUD rounds as today.

## Open questions

1. **`dryTank` and `event.dry_tank`.** (a) Keep the flag and milestone, rewrite
   the line to teach the new rule ("Tank is dry. Land and it refills."), or
   (b) drop the flag, the milestone in `game.js` and the three strings. (a)
   keeps a useful first-time hint; (b) removes a field no longer needed.
2. **Refill underwater?** Grounded on a pool floor refills by the plain rule.
   Accept, or require `!inLiquid`?
3. **Exhaust flame.** It lights whenever airborne with a part tank, including
   free fall. Fix it to "thrusting this frame" as part of C1, or leave it?
4. **Refill while thrusting on the ground** (e.g. under a ceiling): proposed
   no. Confirm.
5. **Help text wording** for `help.s8` in en/pl/uk — approve the proposed
   sentence or supply your own.

## Decisions (approved 2026-10-18)

1. Keep `dryTank` and `event.dry_tank`; rewrite the line to teach "land to refuel".
2. Standing on a pool floor refuels.
3. The exhaust flame shows only while thrusting this frame.
4. Thrusting while grounded blocks the refill.
5. New `help.s8` wording is reviewed at the code step.
