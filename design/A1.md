# A1: Pickup zone

Status: docs, rewritten 2026-10-18 as a behaviour spec. Feedback items 1, 2,
3, 7 (A2 merged in).

## 1. Purpose

The drone sells whatever cargo the player gathers in the pickup zone. In the
first playtest the zone was a rectangle dragged anywhere, and players said:

- it was tiny, and it was not clear how big it should be;
- the preview went red with no explanation;
- it was drawn in the sand colour, so on sand it was invisible;
- it could be dragged over sand, ore and under rock, so building it destroyed
  resources and put it where the drone, which comes from the sky, could never
  reach.

The pickup zone becomes a building of fixed size. The player places it with
one click on open ground under open sky, sees it clearly against any terrain,
and is told in words why a placement or a drone call is refused. Cargo heaped
on it is what the drone sells.

## 2. Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| cell | The smallest piece of the world: one material at one position. 1 cell = 1 cm. |
| empty | A cell holding nothing or a gas (air, smoke, steam, fire). |
| support | Any cell that is neither empty nor liquid: ground, rock, a powder grain, a structure. |
| loose material | Material that falls and slides: powders such as sand, soil, coal, ore grains. |
| liquid | Material that flows: water, lava, molten metal or glass. |
| fixed thing | Material that stays where it is without support: rock, bedrock, solid metal or glass, any building (wall, belt, the zone itself). |
| pickup zone | The building. Made of cells, like everything in the world: a platform and its piles. At most one exists. |
| platform | The floor of the zone: a horizontal slab of zone cells. |
| pile | A vertical column of zone cells from the platform's underside down to the first support below it. |
| pile position | A place along the platform where a pile stands: both ends and every pile-spacing cells between. |
| cargo box | The region directly above the platform, as wide as the platform and cargo-box-height tall. Not made of cells; only its contents matter. |
| column above the platform | Every cell above the platform, across its full width, up to the top of the world. It contains the cargo box and, above it, the sky column. |
| sky column | The part of the column above the platform that lies above the cargo box. |
| sky blocker | A fixed thing in the column being checked. Loose material, liquid and gas never block. |
| world window | The part of the world around the player that is simulated and visible. Outside it the world is stored unchanged and does not move. |
| drone | Flies in from the sky after a paid call, takes the priced cargo from the cargo box and pays for it. |
| current price | What one cell of a good sells for at this moment; goods without a price (soil, slag, water) are not cargo. |
| zone colour | The one colour of the built zone, its cargo box outline, the drone and the drone's beam. |

## 3. Parameters

All values are tuned in play; tests use the names, never the numbers.

| Name | Unit | Start value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| platform width | cells | 40 (0.4 m) | Horizontal size of the platform. |
| platform thickness | cells | 3 (0.03 m) | Vertical size of the platform. |
| pile width | cells | 2 (0.02 m) | Horizontal size of each pile. |
| pile spacing | cells | 10 (0.1 m) | Distance between neighbouring pile positions. |
| max height | cells | 30 (0.3 m) | Longest pile allowed at placement. |
| cargo box height | cells | 20 (0.2 m) | Height of the cargo box. Must be at least half the platform width, rounded up (R11). |
| drone fee | money | as today | Paid on each call, refunded on abort. |

## 4. Rules

**R1 One zone.** At most one pickup zone exists in the world at any time.

**R2 Shape.** A zone is a platform of platform width by platform thickness
cells, plus one pile at each pile position. Pile positions are: the left end
(pile flush with the platform's left edge), every pile spacing cells to the
right of it while a whole pile still fits, and the right end (pile flush with
the right edge). A pile is pile width wide and as long as the empty gap
between the platform's underside and the first support below that pile; a
pile with no gap has length zero and adds no cells.

**R3 Anchor and settling.** The clicked column becomes the platform's middle
column (the left one of the two middle columns when the width is even). The
platform starts with its bottom row on the clicked row and is lowered, as one
rigid piece, while every cell directly below its full width is empty. It
stops at the first row where any cell directly below it is support.

**R4 Room.** Placement needs every platform cell, at its settled position, to
be empty and inside the world window.

**R5 Height.** Placement needs every pile to reach support within max height
cells. If any pile would be longer, or the platform finds no support below it
inside the world window, placement is refused as too high. Loose material
counts as support; liquid does not, so a pile passes through water to the
bottom. A platform that settles into liquid is refused as no room (R4).

**R6 Sky at placement.** Placement needs no sky blocker anywhere in the
column above the platform (cargo box and sky column). Cells above the top of
the world count as open sky.

**R7 Placement.** One click places the zone. It is free. The checks run in
this order and the first that fails is the only one reported: R1, R4, R6, R5.
When all pass, every platform and pile cell becomes a zone cell at once.
Nothing else in the world changes. The player's body is not a cell and never
blocks placement.

**R8 Integrity.** Zone cells change only by removal (R9). Drilling, heat,
fire, flowing or falling material, other tools and other buildings never
change, move or replace them. Support is checked only at placement: if the
cells under the platform or a pile disappear later, the zone stays as it is.

**R9 Removal.** Right-clicking any zone cell removes the whole zone: every
platform and pile cell becomes empty at once, wherever it lies, and whatever
rested on it falls. Afterwards no zone exists and a new one may be placed.
There is no other way to move a zone.

**R10 Bare surface.** The zone has no side walls and its surface does not
hold material. Loose material on the platform slides exactly as on bare
ground and forms a natural heap; what does not fit falls off the edges.
Liquid runs off the edges.

**R11 Cargo box covers the heap.** Loose material rests at a slope of one
cell sideways per cell up, so the tallest heap the platform can hold is half
the platform width, rounded up. Cargo box height is at least that. Loose
material poured onto the platform therefore never rises above the cargo box
and never enters the sky column.

**R12 Drone call.** A call succeeds only when, checked in this order: a zone
exists; its platform and cargo box lie fully inside the world window; the sky
column holds no sky blocker; the player has the drone fee; no drone is
already flying. The first failing check refuses the call and says why
(except "already flying", which is ignored). On success the fee is paid and
the drone sets out.

**R13 Drone approach.** From the call until the pickup, if at any moment one
of the first three conditions of R12 fails (zone removed, zone outside the
window, sky column blocked), the drone turns back, the fee is refunded and
the player is told why.

**R14 Sale.** At the pickup the drone takes every priced cell inside the
cargo box and pays its current price. Cells without a price stay. Nothing
outside the cargo box is taken. If nothing was priced, the player is told
the drone came back empty.

**R15 Persistence.** The zone, with whatever lies on it, survives the world
window moving away and back. Starting a new world leaves no zone.

**R16 Preview.** While the zone is the chosen building, hovering shows the
whole building (platform and every pile) at the position R3 would settle it,
tinted "ok" when a click now would place it under R7 and "bad" otherwise.
Nothing else, such as reach or money, changes the tint. When the tint is bad,
the refusal reason a click would give is shown next to the cursor.

**R17 Visibility.** The zone colour contrasts with sand, soil and rock. The
built platform and piles, the cargo box outline, the drone and its beam use
the zone colour. Both preview tints also contrast with sand.

## 5. States and transitions

Zone states are read from the world each time, never stored apart from the
zone's cells.

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| none | No zone exists. |
| ready | Zone exists, inside the window, sky column clear. |
| blocked | Zone exists, inside the window, a sky blocker is in the sky column. |
| away | Zone exists, part of its platform or cargo box is outside the window. |

| From | Event | To | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | click, all checks pass (R7) | ready | Zone cells appear. |
| none | click, a check fails | none | Refusal (section 6). |
| ready, blocked, away | click to place | same | Refused: zone already exists. |
| ready, blocked | right-click a zone cell | none | Zone cells vanish; resting material falls; a flying drone turns back with refund. |
| ready | a fixed thing appears in the sky column | blocked | Calls refused; a flying drone turns back with refund. |
| blocked | the sky column clears | ready | Calls allowed again. |
| ready, blocked | window moves so the zone leaves it | away | Calls refused; a flying drone turns back with refund. |
| away | window returns over the zone | ready or blocked | Zone and its cargo are as they were. |
| any | new world | none | — |

Drone, seen from the zone:

| From | Event | To | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | call, R12 passes | approaching | Fee paid. |
| idle | call, R12 fails | idle | Refusal. |
| approaching | zone leaves ready | idle | Fee refunded, reason told. |
| approaching | reaches the zone while ready | returning | Priced cargo in the box taken and paid (R14). |
| returning | trip ends | idle | — |

## 6. Refusals

| Event | Reason | Player hears / sees |
|---|---|---|
| place | a zone already exists (R1) | Denied sound; "A pickup zone already exists — remove it to build another." |
| place | a platform cell is not empty or outside the window (R4) | Denied sound; "No room here." |
| place | sky blocker above the platform (R6) | Denied sound; "Something blocks the sky above — the drone could not get through." |
| place | a pile longer than max height, or no support (R5) | Denied sound; "Too high above the ground." |
| call drone | no zone | Denied sound; "Build a pickup zone first." |
| call drone | zone outside the window | Denied sound; "The pickup zone is too far away." |
| call drone | sky column blocked | Denied sound; "Something blocks the sky above the pickup zone." |
| call drone | not enough money | Denied sound; "You need (fee) to call the drone." |
| call drone | drone already flying | Nothing. |
| drone approaching | zone removed, away or blocked (R13) | Denied sound; "The drone turned back: (reason) — fee refunded." |
| drone pickup | no priced cargo in the box | Denied sound; "The drone came back empty." |

The drone status line in the interface shows the same reason as a call
refusal whenever a call would be refused.

## 7. Acceptance scenarios

Each scenario names the rules it proves. "Flat ground" means a row of rock
with empty cells above it to the top of the world, wider than the platform.

**A1 Place on flat ground** (R2, R3, R7). Given flat ground and no zone,
when the player clicks one cell above the ground, then a zone exists whose
platform lies directly on the ground, centred on the clicked column, has the
platform size, and has no pile cells.

**A2 Settle from the air** (R3). Given flat ground, when the player clicks
high above it, then the platform ends up directly on the ground, not where
the cursor was.

**A3 Cursor inside the ground** (R3, R4). Given flat ground, when the player
clicks a ground cell, then placement is refused with "No room here" and
nothing changes.

**A4 Platform over a trench** (R2, R3, R5). Given flat ground with a trench
under part of the platform's span, shallower than max height, when the player
places the zone, then the platform rests on the ground beside the trench and
every pile position over the trench has a pile reaching the trench floor.

**A5 Too deep** (R5, R7). Given the same trench deeper than max height under
a pile position, when the player clicks, then placement is refused with "Too
high above the ground" and nothing changes.

**A6 Loose support, liquid passed through** (R5). Given a platform span over
sand on one side and a shallow pool on the other, the pool floor within max
height, when the player places the zone, then it is placed, one pile ends on
the sand and the other passes through the water to the pool floor.

**A7 Gas counts as empty** (R4, R6). Given smoke and steam in the platform
cells and the column above, when the player clicks, then the zone is placed.

**A8 Overhang** (R6). Given one rock cell anywhere in the column above one
platform column, when the player clicks, then placement is refused with the
blocked-sky message. Given the same rock one column outside the platform's
width, the zone is placed.

**A9 Loose material above does not block** (R6). Given sand falling through
the column above the platform, when the player clicks, then the zone is
placed.

**A10 Out of the window** (R4). Given a click near the window edge such that
part of the platform would lie outside the window, then placement is refused
with "No room here".

**A11 One zone** (R1, R7). Given a zone exists, when the player clicks a
valid site elsewhere, then it is refused with "already exists" and the
existing zone is unchanged.

**A12 Check order** (R7). Given a zone exists and the new site also has a
blocked sky, when the player clicks, then only "already exists" is reported.

**A13 Removal** (R9). Given a zone with sand heaped on it, when the player
right-clicks a pile cell, then no zone cell remains anywhere, the sand falls,
no zone exists, and a new zone can be placed at once.

**A14 Removal reaches stored cells** (R9, R15). Given a zone partly outside
the window, when the player right-clicks a visible zone cell, then after the
window moves over the rest no zone cell remains.

**A15 Ground dug away** (R8). Given a zone on sand piles, when the sand
under the platform and piles is removed, then every zone cell stays where it
was.

**A16 Indestructible** (R8). Given a zone, when the player drills a zone
cell, lava flows against it and sand falls on it for a long time, then every
zone cell is unchanged.

**A17 Overflow stays in the box** (R10, R11, R14). Given a zone on flat
ground with empty space beside it, when sand is poured onto the middle of the
platform until it spills off both edges and keeps pouring, then sand lands
on the ground beside the platform, no sand cell resting on the platform is
above the cargo box, the sky column holds no sand at rest, and the zone is
ready.

**A18 Liquid runs off** (R10). Given a zone, when water is poured onto it,
then after it settles no water remains on the platform.

**A19 Call refusals** (R12). Given no zone, a call is refused with "Build a
pickup zone first". Given a zone outside the window, it is refused with "too
far away". Given a wall built across the sky column, it is refused with the
blocked-sky message. In each case money and drone state are unchanged.

**A20 Wall inside the box does not block** (R12). Given a zone with a small
wall standing on the platform entirely inside the cargo box, when the player
calls the drone, then the call succeeds.

**A21 Abort on blocked sky** (R13). Given a drone approaching, when a fixed
thing appears in the sky column before the pickup, then the drone turns back,
the fee is refunded and the player hears why.

**A22 Abort on removal or leaving** (R13). Given a drone approaching, when
the zone is removed, or the window moves so the zone leaves it, then the
drone turns back with a refund.

**A23 Sale** (R14). Given iron grains and soil in the cargo box and iron
grains just above the box and just beside the platform, when the drone
arrives, then exactly the iron inside the box is removed and paid at the
current iron price, and the soil and the outside iron remain.

**A24 Persistence** (R15). Given a zone with cargo, when the window moves
away until the zone is outside it and back, then the zone is away meanwhile
and afterwards the zone and its cargo are where they were.

**A25 New world** (R15). Given a zone, when a new world starts, then no zone
exists.

**A26 Preview tint** (R16). Given the zone chosen, hovering a valid site
tints the shown building ok; hovering under an overhang, too high, or while a
zone exists tints it bad and shows that refusal reason next to the cursor. The tint is the same whether or not the player is
within reach and whatever their money.

**A27 Preview shape** (R16). Given the zone chosen and a trench under part of
the span, hovering shows the platform at its settled position and the piles
down to the trench floor.

R17 is checked by eye in the browser.

## 8. Decisions

2026-10-18, by the user:

1. The zone is a building of fixed size, made of cells: platform and piles.
2. One click places it; free. One zone; a second placement is refused. No
   relocation: remove with right-click (cargo falls), build again.
3. Platform cells must be empty (void or gas). The whole column above the
   platform must be clear of fixed things; loose material and cargo never
   block.
4. The platform may stand above ground up to max height on piles at both
   ends and every pile spacing; any support counts, sand included.
5. Support is checked only at placement; collapse is out of scope.
6. The click picks the column; the platform settles onto the support below.
7. No side walls, no grip; cargo box height is at least the largest natural
   heap, so cargo cannot block the sky.
8. Drone needs a zone, inside the window, with a clear sky column; each
   refusal says why. Blocked sky during the approach aborts with a refund.
   Unpriced material stays.
9. Preview shows the full building, tinted only by these rules; the zone,
   drone and beam share one colour that contrasts with sand.

2026-10-19, by the user:

10. Settling is rigid: the platform stops at the first support under any part
    of its width.
11. A click on a non-empty cell is refused as no room; the platform does not
    climb.
12. The drone checks only the sky column; at placement the cargo box is part
    of the checked column.
13. Pile width is its own parameter.
14. Liquid is not support: piles pass through it to the bottom.
15. Settling distance from the click is unlimited; only piles are capped.
16. Solid cargo that cools above the box may block the sky; the drone says so.
17. A bad preview shows its refusal reason next to the cursor.
