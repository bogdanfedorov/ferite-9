# D1 — Fewer notices

Status: docs. Feedback 4 (first playtest, 2026-10-17).

## What and why

Players say there are too many messages on screen. Most of them repeat what
the player has just done and can already see: a belt they placed, a heat map
they turned on, a tool they bought. They also stack: holding the ignite
button out of reach repeats the same line every frame.

Rule from the user: **a notice never confirms an action the player just
performed.** The sound may stay; the text goes. Text is kept for refusals
(the action did not happen, and why), for results that arrive later (drone
sale), and for story lines.

## Current behaviour

There is one text channel: the comms panel (`#comms`, `lore.js`), bottom-left,
above the build bar. Nothing else on screen prints messages. The right side
holds the market panel (`hud.js`): money, prices, cargo in the zone and the
drone button label. It is status, not a message stream, but it changes every
frame and sits opposite the comms panel, which is likely the "right" in the
feedback (see open questions).

Paths into the comms panel:

- Core and Mixed files call `notice(sound, text)` (`notice.js`). The shell hook
  `showNotice` (`game.js:263`) plays `sound` and calls `transmit(text, NOTICE)`.
- Shell files call `transmit(text, NOTICE)` and `play()` directly.
- Story: `loreEvent` (`lore.js:451`, once per key) and the post-boot death line
  (`lore.js:224`) call `transmit` with their own `hold`.

`transmit` (`lore.js:393`):

- `NOTICE = { now: true, hold: 1200 }`. On screen for `hold + 16 ms × length`,
  so an 80-character notice stays about 2.5 s, after a typewriter reveal.
- `now` puts the message at the queue front and ends the current one
  (`comms.hold = 0`). The interrupted line is dropped, not requeued, so a
  build confirmation can erase a story line half-way.
- `repeatsShown`: an identical text already on screen only extends its hold;
  identical text at the queue front is dropped. No counter, so the player
  sees one line that never goes away while the action repeats.
- Queue capped at 4.

## Proposed behaviour

1. **No echo.** Own-action rows above send `notice(sound, null)` (core) or
   only `play()` (shell). Build specs keep `done` with `text: null`;
   `reportBuild` passes `null` instead of `t(null)`. Unused keys are deleted
   from all three language files.
2. **One line per repeat, with a counter.** A notice whose text equals the one
   on screen or waiting at the queue front is merged: its count goes up, the
   panel shows `text ×N`, and its hold restarts. It is never queued twice.
   Per-frame sources (`msg.tooFar`) count once per press, not per frame:
   a repeat within the current hold does not bump the counter unless the
   previous showing had ended (open question).
3. **Shorter on screen.** Notices: no typewriter reveal, hold about
   `900 ms + 8 ms × length` (an 80-character line ~1.5 s, down from ~2.5 s).
   Story lines keep today's timing.
4. **Notices do not erase story.** A notice shown with `now` puts the
   interrupted story line back at the queue front instead of dropping it.

## Core and shell

- Core (`market.js`, `Toolbelt.js`, `Build.js`, `Buildings/*.js`): only the
  removals in step 1. `notice(sound, text)` keeps its signature; `text: null`
  is already legal. Tests: `test/notice.test.js` expectations change to
  `['unlock', null]` for buying, `['launch', null]` for the drone call, and a
  build `done` report reaches the hook as `['built', null]`. `test/silent.test.js`
  needs no change.
- Shell (`game.js`, `input.js`, `lore.js`): removals in step 1, and steps 2–4,
  which live in the comms queue. Today that queue is shell-only and has no
  unit test. Proposal: move the queue model (`queue`, current message,
  `count`, merge rule, hold arithmetic, per-tick countdown) into a small core
  file, keeping DOM writes in `lore.js`. Then the counter and the timing are
  stated by headless tests like the rest of the core.

## Open questions

1. Which messages did the players mean on the **right**? Only the comms panel
   (bottom-left) prints messages. Is the market panel part of D1, or is it
   covered by D5 (prices move to their own view)?
2. `msg.soundOff` from the mute hotkey: muting has no audible feedback and no
   HUD indicator outside Settings. Keep this one text, or add an indicator?
3. `msg.nothingToRemove` today is text with no sound. Replace with the
   `denied` sound only, or keep the text?
4. Counter rule for held actions: count once per press (needs the press edge
   passed to `notice`, or a short merge window in the comms queue) or count
   every call (a held ignite would show `×60` in a second)?
5. Notice timing: are `900 ms + 8 ms × length` and no typewriter acceptable,
   or should the number be a constant tuned in the browser?
6. Move the comms queue into a core file for testing (proposed), or keep it in
   `lore.js` and check steps 2–4 in the browser only?
7. `msg.nothingToGrab` is the longest notice (~85 characters) and repeats on
   every click. Shorten it as part of D1?
8. `msg.beltRoom` is used by every building, not only belts. Rename the key
   while touching the language files, or leave it?

## Decisions (approved 2026-10-18)

1. "Messages on the right" is the market panel, covered by D5.
2. `msg.soundOff` keeps its text until D6 adds a sound button.
3. `msg.nothingToRemove` becomes the `denied` sound only.
4. A held action counts once per press.
5. Notice timing is a constant tuned in the browser.
6. The comms queue logic moves into a core file with unit tests.
7. Shorten `msg.nothingToGrab`.
8. Rename `msg.beltRoom` to `msg.noRoom`.
