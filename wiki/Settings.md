# Settings

`O`, or the settings button on the module plate at the top left. The mine pauses while
the panel is open. Everything is remembered between shifts.

## Language

Ukrainian, English, Polish. The same switch as on the title card — it changes
everything at once, including lines Mission Control has already sent.

## Sound

**Mute** kills every beep; `M` does the same from the game. **Volume** scales all of them
together, so the drill stays quieter than the shuttle.

## Grab size

How much the hand closes on, from 1×1 to 4×4 cells — that is 1 to 16 cells per
fistful.

A small grab is surgical: it picks a couple of grains out of a mixed spoil heap.
A large one carries a shovelful per trip, which is what you want once the
furnace is dug and you are just feeding it. Either way the hand takes only the
material under the cursor, and the highlight shows the exact cells before you
press.

## Colour scheme

Five schemes. **Standard** is the mine as drawn.

**Protanopia**, **Deuteranopia** and **Tritanopia** do not swap in a fixed
palette — they take every colour in the game, work out how much of it is lost to
that kind of colour blindness, and push exactly that much back into the channels
you can still tell apart. Ferrite against soil, embers against slag, the green
power light against the red one: pairs that used to merge come apart along an
axis that reads.

**High contrast** leaves hues alone and pulls saturation and contrast up. It
helps on a washed-out screen or in a bright room.

A scheme covers the whole game at once — rock, molten metal, the heat map, the
status bars, the belt markers — so nothing is left in the old palette to confuse
matters. The swatch strip on each tile is the real transform of ferrite, coal,
iron, glass, lava and water, so you can pick one by looking rather than by name.

## Keys

Every action in the list can be rebound: click the key, press the new one.
Escape cancels instead of binding. A key already in use is taken from whoever
had it, so nothing ends up doing two things at once — an action left with no key
shows a dash. **Defaults** puts the whole list back.

Movement ships with two or three keys per direction (WASD, the arrows, and space
for the jetpack). Rebinding replaces all of them with the single key you press.

Mouse controls are fixed: left button is the tool, right is the grapple, and
the wheel zooms.
