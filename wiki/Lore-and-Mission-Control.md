# Lore and Mission Control

You are a survey module, `sol_{revision}.{drop number}`, dropped into the mine
by Mission Control "Sol". Memory does not carry over between drops — too
expensive — so Mission Control explains everything again every time, and gets
more sarcastic about it each round.

Ways to lose the module, and what you get told:

| Cause | Condition | Sample line |
|---|---|---|
| Melted | contact with lava | "Lava is hot. Melt away. Logged: hot." |
| Overheated | above 120 °C around the module | "You have a thermal probe. Attention is the open question." |
| Fall | more than 17 cells down | "Fall height, 52 cells. The jetpack was right there." |
| Drill burnout | drilling rock hotter than 260 °C | "Drill into red-hot rock, then act surprised that it hurts." |
| Buried | fully covered by loose material for 3 seconds | "Sand on the head. Painful." |

The first three deaths from the same cause have their own lines, escalating
("Lava again. Why didn't we keep the note about it hurting?"), after which you
get a random line from the mocking pool with the loss statistics attached. Every
death increments the drop number; after the tenth, Mission Control ships a new
hull revision.

Besides deaths the channel comments on firsts: masonry, the first coal catching,
the first melt, the first ingot, glass and cargo being ready.
