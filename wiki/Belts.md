# Builder

Mission Control releases the **Builder** (4) once the shift quota is closed.
It works by dragging: hold the left button and pull. Press **R** while it is
selected to cycle what it places — belt, wall line, or wall block. Nothing it
places costs any material.

**Belt**: the line snaps either **level** or **exactly 45°** — a belt knows
nothing in between. Whichever way you dragged is the way it will carry, shown
by an arrow on every cell of decking, both while dragging and once built. The
track has to be clear, with no stone directly above it. Cargo rides on top of
the decking; sides hold it in place, so on an incline it does not slide back
but climbs — one cell every three frames.

**Wall line / wall block**: lays a straight run of wall, or fills the dragged
rectangle solid. Walls block movement and drilling like rock but go up and
come down instantly.

A short drag (a click) removes whatever is under the cursor: a whole belt run,
or a single wall cell.
