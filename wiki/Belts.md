# Belts

The first thing the module builds out of its own iron rather than out of rock.
Mission Control releases the blueprint once the shift quota is closed.

The **Belt** tool (4) works by dragging: hold the left button and pull, and the
line snaps either **level** or **exactly 45°** — a belt knows nothing in
between. Whichever way you dragged is the way it will carry; the arrow at the
end shows the direction. The frame goes up empty in mid-air: the track has to be
clear, and there must be no stone directly above it.

Then you **feed it iron by hand**. Every cell of scrap you bring lays four cells
of decking, so the belt visibly grows from start to end and begins carrying
before it is finished. Clicking a frame takes it down and returns what was not
spent; clicking a finished belt breaks it back into scrap — exactly as much as
it cost.

Cargo rides on top of the decking. Sides hold it in place, so on an incline it
does not slide back but climbs — one cell every three frames.
