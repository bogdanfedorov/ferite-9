# Smelting iron

1. **The drill** chews through rock on a timer: sand is instant, stone takes
   5 ticks, ferrite 4, iron 7. Cracks on a cell show the work done so far.
   It does not touch liquids at all — the beam goes straight through water and
   lava to the rock behind them. Liquid does not vanish when you build either:
   masonry, a pipe or a machine pushes it into the nearest free cell, and if
   there is nowhere to push it, you cannot build there.
2. **Dig the furnace out of stone**: drill a niche with a narrow mouth. Stone
   holds heat and air carries it away, so solid walls matter.
3. **Carry coal and ferrite in by hand** — coal at the bottom, ore above it —
   then hit **Ignite** on the coal. Coal turns into embers: 1500 °C, burning for
   about 50 seconds.
4. Ferrite melts at **1120 °C**, seeps down through the hearth and collects on
   the floor as liquid iron. Sand melts at 1340 °C and gives glass.
5. The melt sets below **850 °C**, or instantly on water — quenching gives off
   steam. Latent heat keeps it flowing for about two more seconds after it
   cools, so you can cast it down a channel several dozen cells long.
6. Break the finished ingots out with the drill. One charge of 39 cells of coal
   gives roughly 39 ingots.

## Rock has two states, and there is no inventory

Every rock exists in two states: **as it lies** (solid, stays put) and **loose**
(granular). The drill only works on the first, the hand only on the second.

| As it lies | Under the drill becomes |
|---|---|
| stone | gravel |
| hardpan | dirt |
| coal seam | coal |
| ferrite vein | ferrite |
| iron (ingot) | iron scrap |
| glass | cullet |

Sand is loose from the start — the hand takes it straight out of the dune.

What you break out does not go into any bag: it **spills right there in the
world** and has to be carried by hand from then on. Rock compacts in the
process — two broken cells give one loose cell — otherwise your own spoil would
block the drift immediately.

There is no inventory at all. The shift goal is delivered physically: the
**cargo pad** by the airlock takes the iron scrap and cullet the module brings
to it. Anything that falls inside its bounds is picked up by orbit at once.
