# Glass needs a different stack

Sand does not melt until **1340 °C**, and that is not a balance whim but a
consequence of diffusion: a cell with one hot neighbour settles at around
**1240 °C**, even though the embers hold 1500. So a layer of sand laid **on top
of** coal heats forever and never runs — measured, the ceiling is exactly
1236 °C.

To get glass, sand has to be **buried inside the coal**. When embers surround a
cell on three or four sides, the equilibrium rises above the melting point. On
the test bench a pocket of 15 cells of sand inside a coal body melted completely
in 10 seconds. The stacking order is simple: a layer of coal, a narrow strip of
sand, more coal on top.

If the player heats sand pointlessly for long enough, Mission Control mentions
it in the channel.

Hence the difference between the metals: **iron is taken in layers, glass only
by burial**.
