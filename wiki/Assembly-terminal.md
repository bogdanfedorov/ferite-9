# Assembly terminal

Every life starts at the Mission Control console. Death lands here too, not in
the side channel: the terminal opens with a loss report, telemetry (fall height
against tolerance, temperature against tolerance) and a sarcastic remark you
have to **acknowledge by typing** — `ENTER`, "accept report".

Then comes the hull assembly log, the readout of the previous drop's memory, and
the decision to discard it ("integrity 12% · fragments: 'hot', 'sand' · memory
discarded"). The fragments come from the real causes of your deaths, and the
shift task shows the current delivery progress.

## The terminal never repeats itself

What changes:

- the channel header and two or three assembly lines from the general pool;
- a line tied to the cause of the last write-off — after lava it is "plating ·
  rated 120 °C · request for 1200 denied", after a cave-in "collapse sensor ·
  out of stock", after a fall "shock absorbers · removed to save weight";
- the wording of reading and discarding the memory;
- from the third death on, a recommendation based on your most frequent cause
  with a counter ("stay out of lava · 3rd repeat") and the note "recommendation
  status · not carried into memory";
- a progress line: if the delivery has not moved since the last drop, Mission
  Control points it out, and if iron is already past half the quota — "pace ·
  unexpectedly acceptable".

## Basic test

It all ends with a basic test in the form of a call and response: Mission
Control gives a reference line, the module repeats it. The words are its own,
miner's words — "rock", "bonded" — closing with "Rock in rock is bonded".
You answer with `ENTER` or a click; `ESC` skips.

From the fourth death the test gets shorter, and after that the module starts
slipping: on the 5th it adds "It was hot" to the correct answer, on the 9th
"I've said this already. Nine times", and on the 15th Mission Control cuts the
test short.
