# Interface

The HUD sits in the corners so the middle of the screen stays the game.

- **top left, the module**: callsign, hull integrity and jetpack fuel (bars go
  green, yellow, red), the settings and help buttons, and below them — only in
  heat map mode — the temperature scale;
- **top right, the market**: your money, the price of each good with a bar that
  shrinks as the price falls, how much of each lies in the pickup zone, and the
  drone button. The button says what is missing when it is disabled;
- **bottom left, the Mission Control channel**; bottom centre, the 8-cell
  hotbar (keys 1–8; **E** opens the menu of every tool, where you place them
  into cells);
- **the thermal probe follows the cursor**, so you read rock and temperature
  where you are looking instead of in a corner. Near the edge of the window the
  chip flips to the other side.

The hotbar keeps a single row down to 640 px wide, then wraps. Below 980 px the settings and help buttons show only their key, below 640 px the hint
line is hidden.

The module plate shows the percentage of the world explored.
